Who We Are

PAG Tutors is a professional accountancy training centre founded in 2006. We specialize in preparing students for the Institute of Chartered Accountants of Nigeria (ICAN) examinations at both ATS and Professional levels. Our lecturers are highly experienced ICAN members and practicing accountants.

Our Mission

To produce competent, ethical, and exam-ready accountants who can compete globally and seamlessly handle complex corporate financial demands.

Our Vision

To be the leading ICAN tuition house in South-West Nigeria, bridging the gap between basic academic knowledge and professional marketplace success.
